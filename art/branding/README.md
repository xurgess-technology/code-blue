# Branding

## St. Doe's General Hospital (in-world)
- `st_does_logo.jpg`: the approved hospital logo (2026-09-17). A resting doe with a brass halo,
  doe-teal on chart paper, serif wordmark. Shortened to "Doe General" on faxes.

## Malpractice (the game)
- `malpractice_banner.webp`: the banner logo. The pill bottle on its side, full prescription label.
- `malpractice_icon.webp`: the square icon on a dark teal background (MAL / PRAC / TICE).
- `malpractice_icon_transparent.jpeg`: the same icon exported "transparent" — but JPEG has no alpha,
  so its checkerboard is painted in. Kept as the source for the cut-out below.
- `malpractice_icon_transparent.png`: real transparency, cut from the JPEG by flood-filling the
  checkerboard and trimming to the bottle (1024x1024, square, small padding).
- `malpractice_icon_512.png`, `icon.ico`: sizes derived from that PNG.

`../../icon.png` (256x256) and `../../icon.ico` (16-256) are the shipped app icons, generated from
`malpractice_icon_transparent.png`. `project.godot` uses them for the window and taskbar icon
(`config/icon`, `config/windows_native_icon`) and `export_presets.cfg` for the .exe icon.

All of these were generated by Zach in Gemini (the originals carry the Gemini watermark) from
prompts written in chat. They are references and placeholders: a clean redraw is needed before
release, and any shipped art needs an ASSETS.md entry.

`.gdignore` keeps Godot from importing this folder.
